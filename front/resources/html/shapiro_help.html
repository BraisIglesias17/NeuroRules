Shapiro-Wilk Test

Description: The Shapiro-Wilk test is a normality test used to determine if a sample follows a normal distribution.

Preconditions:

  • The sample must be random.
  • The sample must be independent and identically distributed (iid).

Implications and Consequences:

  • If the p-value is less than the chosen significance level (e.g., 0.05), the null hypothesis of normality is rejected.
