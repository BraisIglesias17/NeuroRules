Pearson Correlation Test

Description: Pearson correlation evaluates the strength and direction of the linear relationship between two continuous variables.

Preconditions:

  • Both variables must be continuous.
  • Variables should be approximately normally distributed.

Implications and Consequences:

  • The correlation coefficient varies between -1 and 1.
  • A value close to 1 indicates a strong positive correlation, while a value close to -1 indicates a strong negative correlation.
